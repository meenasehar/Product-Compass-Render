What changed from the current design
1
Metric Strip (3-card row duplicating Cover Story numbers) removed entirely
2
Delivery ring now scoped to v7.0 features only, not all 21 across all releases
3
At-risk section promoted to full-width, immediately after stats — before any charts
4
4th stat card shows "Days to Q4 Close" — a new, genuinely distinct metric Morgan can act on
5
Pillar charts moved to bottom — they answer "how is work distributed," not "should I be worried"
Option B Risk-first when issues exist. When atRisk.length > 0, the page opens with an "Action Required" hero zone — at-risk features front and center. Delivery progress is secondary, beneath. When all features are healthy (atRisk = 0), the delivery ring would lead instead. This is honest and urgent but may feel alarming in a context where the exec already expects risks to exist.
